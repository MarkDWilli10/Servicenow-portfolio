# Project 04 – Change Management

## Objective

Demonstrate the Change Management process in ServiceNow using ITIL best practices to safely plan, approve, implement, and review changes.

---

## Skills Demonstrated

- Change Management
- Risk Assessment
- Change Approval
- Change Scheduling
- Implementation Planning
- Rollback Planning
- Post-Implementation Review
- Documentation

---

## What is Change Management?

Change Management ensures that changes to IT services are planned, tested, approved, and implemented with minimal risk to the business.

---

## Change Scenario

A company needs to update all employee laptops to the latest VPN software version.

---

## Change Type

Normal Change

---

## Process

1. Submit a Change Request.
2. Describe the business reason for the change.
3. Assess the impact on users and systems.
4. Evaluate potential risks.
5. Develop an implementation plan.
6. Create a rollback plan in case the update fails.
7. Obtain manager or CAB approval.
8. Schedule the maintenance window.
9. Implement the change.
10. Verify successful completion.
11. Perform a Post-Implementation Review (PIR).
12. Close the Change Request.

---

## Risk Level

Medium

---

## Approval Required

- IT Manager
- Change Advisory Board (CAB)

---

## Rollback Plan

If the VPN update fails:

- Remove the new VPN software.
- Reinstall the previous version.
- Verify users can reconnect.
- Notify stakeholders.

---

## Expected Outcome

All employee laptops are successfully updated without disrupting business operations.

---

## Lessons Learned

A successful change requires planning, communication, testing, approvals, and a documented rollback strategy.
