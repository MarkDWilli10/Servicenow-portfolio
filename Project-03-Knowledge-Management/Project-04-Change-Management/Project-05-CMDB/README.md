## What is a CMDB?

A Configuration Management Database (CMDB) stores information about the hardware, software, services, and infrastructure that make up an organization's IT environment.

It helps IT teams understand how systems are connected and what may be affected when changes or incidents occur.

---

- Databases

- Cloud Resources

---

## Example Scenario

A user reports they cannot access the company email system.

The Service Desk checks the CMDB and discovers:

- The user's laptop connects to the corporate network.
- The corporate network connects to the Exchange Server.
- The Exchange Server is currently experiencing an outage.

Because these relationships are documented in the CMDB, the support team quickly identifies the root cause.

## Benefits of a CMDB

- Faster incident resolution
- Better change planning
- Improved impact analysis
- Easier troubleshooting
- Better asset visibility
- Improved compliance

## Lessons Learned

A CMDB is more than an inventory. It shows how Configuration Items are connected, allowing IT teams to understand the impact of incidents and planned changes.
